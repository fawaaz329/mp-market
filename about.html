Our Story: Bringing Trending Finds Straight to Your Door 🇿🇦

MP Market was born out of a hustle to solve a real problem.

We all know those incredibly smart local traders who sell the coolest, most useful imported goods—everything from viral gadgets and homeware hacks to trending lifestyle items. They have fantastic products, but unless you have the time to go out, walk past their stalls, or hunt them down on social media, you miss out on the best deals.

Enter MP Market – Your Digital Bridge

We decided to change the game. We created this webapp to hand-pick the best, fast-selling imported products from local traders and put them into one easy-to-use digital marketplace.

Think of us as your personal sourcing and delivery concierge. We do the running around, check the stalls, verify the items, and handle the logistics so that you can enjoy the most popular trending products without ever leaving your house.

Our 48-Hour Door-to-Door Delivery Promise

Convenience and safety are our absolute top priorities. That is why we operate on a strict delivery-only model:

  • Straight to Your Door: You don't have to travel, commute, or go searching for these items. You order on the app, and we bring it straight to your doorstep.
  • 48-Hour Turnaround: We buy the item directly from the local supplier and deliver it to you within 48 hours. While we are proudly based in Mitchell's Plain, our delivery network extends to surrounding Cape Town areas. (Note: To keep our drivers safe, we only deliver to secure zones. Your delivery availability will be automatically verified once you enter your area at checkout).
  • Zero-Risk Guarantee: If a local seller runs out of stock, we won't make you wait. You get a 100% immediate refund, no questions asked.
We are making trending shopping faster, easier, and safer for our communities. Thank you for supporting a local business that supports our local trading community!
