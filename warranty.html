MP MARKET — OFFICIAL RETURNS, REFUNDS & WARRANTY POLICY

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Buyer’s Remorse & Change of Mind (The 7-Day Cooling-Off Period)

In terms of Section 44 of the ECT Act, online consumers are entitled to a 7-day cooling-off period to cancel a purchase for any reason (including "change of mind").

  • Timeframe: The request must be submitted within 7 calendar days of delivery.
  • Condition of Goods: Returns will strictly only be accepted if the goods are entirely unused, unaltered, in their original, unopened factory packaging, and all security seals or tags are 100% intact.
  • Logistics & Cost: The customer is fully responsible for organizing and paying the courier fees to return the unwanted item to our warehouse.

2. Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Goods (6-Month Implied Warranty)

In accordance with Section 56 of the CPA, all products carry a statutory 6-month implied warranty of quality against inherent manufacturing defects.

  • Your Rights: If an item fails during normal, intended use due to a factory flaw within 6 months, the customer has the legal right to choose a repair, a replacement, or a full refund back to their original payment method.
  • Incorrectly Supplied Goods: If we deliver an item substantially different from what was ordered, we will arrange collection and deliver the correct item at no charge.
  • Timeframe for Processing: Once returned stock is received, formal technical inspections may take up to 5 working days before a refund or replacement is cleared. Approved refunds will reflect in the consumer's account within 7 to 14 working days.

3. Catalog & Category-Specific Exclusions

MP Market will strictly NOT accept returns or warranty claims under the following circumstances:

  • Electronics & Gaming: Software, gaming titles, console accessories, or digital voucher codes are entirely non-returnable once the outer plastic shrink-wrap or security seal is broken. Warranty claims are strictly voided if damage is caused by electrical grid surges, power outages, load shedding, or incorrect voltage connections.
  • Hardware & DIY: Using DIY-rated power tools or hand tools for commercial construction or industrial exploitation voids the 6-month warranty of quality. Consumables (such as drill bits, blades, or sanding discs) cannot be returned once opened.
  • Personal Care & Cosmetics: Due to public health and hygiene regulations, all cosmetics, grooming tools, skin treatments, and hair attachments are strictly non-returnable once the protective sanitary seal or packaging has been unsealed or tampered with.
  • Clothing & Apparel: Garments must be returned unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and with all original factory swing-tags attached. Items showing makeup stains, perfume odors, or fabric stretching will be rejected.
  • Stationery & Office: Partial returns of stationery kits, office packs, or gift bundles are prohibited. Packs must be returned 100% complete.
  • User Negligence & Acts of God: Ordinary wear and tear, willful damage, water logging, drop damage, or failures caused by natural disasters (Force Majeure) are explicitly excluded from coverage.

4. In-House Logistics & Step-On Inspection

Because MP Market manages its own local delivery fleet, our delivery team acts as our official inspection agents. For any claims regarding out-of-the-box physical transit damage (cracked glass, broken shells, or dented frames), the issue should be reported within 48 hours.

Doorstep Verification: Upon pulling up to collect a reported return, our driver will physically inspect the item at your door. MP Market reserves the immediate right to cancel a collection and reject a claim if the item shows undeniable signs of post-delivery user abuse or negligence.
