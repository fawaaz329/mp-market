Customer Protection Policy

MP MARKET — RETURNS & REPLACEMENTS

1. The 7-Day Replacement Guarantee

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or fails within its first week of use, you are covered by our 7-Day Replacement Policy.

Please notify us via WhatsApp or our Contact form within 7 days of delivery. The item must be in its original packaging. We will collect the item and arrange a working replacement for you, or issue a full refund if the item is out of stock.

Standard Return Window: Our official return and fault-reporting window strictly closes 7 days after the delivery date.

2. Non-Returnable Items & Exclusions

For health, hygiene, and logistical reasons, the following items are strictly non-returnable once delivered:

  • Groceries & Food Items: Edible goods, snacks, chocolates, pantry items (e.g., boxed noodles), and beverages.
  • Hygiene & Personal Care: Cosmetics, hair styling tools, clippers, or any items where the factory seal has been broken.
  • User Damage: Electronics damaged by power surges (load-shedding), liquid spills, dropping, or improper use.
  • Clothing: Garments that have been worn, washed, altered, or have had their original tags removed.
  • Consumables: Screen protectors, batteries, or opened stationery packs.

3. Incorrect Items Delivered

If our team accidentally delivers the wrong item, color, or size compared to your digital receipt, please let us know within 48 hours. We will arrange a free collection and swap the item for the correct one at absolutely no cost to you.
