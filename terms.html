1. Personal Concierge & Procurement

MP Market acts strictly as your personal shopper, procurement agent, and delivery concierge. We do not hold physical inventory. By checking out, you authorize MP Market to purchase the selected items from third-party suppliers on your behalf.

2. Pricing and Service Fees

The total price displayed at checkout includes the retail cost of the item and our procurement service fee. By completing the payment, you agree to this total cost for our sourcing services.

3. Out of Stock & Cancellations

If an item is unfulfilled or unavailable at the supplier, your order for that item will be cancelled, and a 100% refund will be issued immediately to your original payment method.

Delivery fees are earned upon the successful execution and completion of the delivery run.

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect from the supplier, we will handle the return, replacement, or refund in accordance with your statutory CPA rights.
