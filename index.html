🇿🇦 MP Market Returns & CPA Policy

1. Consumer Protection Act (CPA) Compliance:

Our Returns Policy operates in strict accordance with the Consumer Protection Act (CPA of 2008) and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (ECTA). Returns can be initiated within 7 to 14 days of order receipt.


2. Circumstances for Returns:

  • Incorrectly Supplied Goods: If you receive an item different from what was ordered, MP Market collects the goods and delivers the correct item at no charge.
  • Materially Defective Goods (6-Month Warranty): If goods fail due to a manufacturing defect, they will be inspected before offering a replacement, store credit, or full refund.

3. Exclusions:

  • Wear and tear, electrical surges, or willful damage.
  • Opened cosmetics, beauty, personal body care items, earrings, or underwear due to public health considerations.
  • Buyer's remorse after sourcing is completed.
