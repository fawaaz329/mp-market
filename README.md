# MP Market — Local Sourcing, Made Simple.

A full-scale ecommerce platform built on Cloudflare Workers, Cloudflare D1, Cloudflare R2, and Cloudflare Durable Objects.

## Features
- **7 Non-Food Sourcing Categories**: Hardware, Electronics, Home, Clothing, Beauty, Gaming, and Gifts.
- **Zero-Inventory Mode**: Dynamic sourcing with 1-click availability control.
- **Takealot-Style Cart**: Dynamic delivery calculation with a live free shipping progress bar.
- **Pre-Wired Yoco Gateway**: Accepts Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, and Google Pay with server-side bank verification.
- **Consumer Protection Act (CPA) Policy**: South African e-commerce compliant legal policy.
- **Real-Time Order Notifications**: WebSockets and Cloudflare Durable Objects for live order alerts.
- **1-Click Dispatch Waybills**: Printable packing slips with `window.print()`.
- **R2 Image Storage**: Product images upload directly from your device into Cloudflare R2.
