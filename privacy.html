Legal & Data Protection

MP MARKET — PRIVACY & POPIA NOTICE

In compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), Act No. 4 of 2013

1. Responsible Party & Commitment

MP Market ("We", "Us", "Our"), operating in Cape Town, South Africa, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information in strict accordance with POPIA.

2. What Information We Collect

To fulfill your online sourcing orders, we collect:

  • Identity & Contact: Full Name, Email Address, and WhatsApp / Cell Phone Number.
  • Delivery Details: Street Address, Suburb, and City for driver dispatch.
  • Transaction Metadata: Order items, selected sizes, and payment status confirmation.

3. Purpose of Processing (Why We Need It)

We process your data strictly under the lawful grounds of contractual necessity to:

  • Source and deliver the physical products you purchased.
  • Send transactional order receipts and dispatch tracking alerts.
  • Coordinate doorstep handover via local delivery drivers.

4. Third-Party Operators & Payment Security

We do not sell or rent your personal information to advertisers. We only share necessary data with trusted operational partners:

  • Yoco Technologies: Processes card transactions securely. MP Market never sees or stores credit card numbers or banking passwords.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue): Delivers automated operational email receipts and status updates.
  • Local Delivery Drivers: Provided only with your delivery address and contact number to complete delivery.

5. Address Masking & Lookup Security

In accordance with Section 19 of POPIA, all order lookups are masked. Full residential street addresses are never publicly revealed on general tracking queries.

6. Your Rights & Data Erasure (POPIA Section 24)

Under POPIA, you have the legal right to request access to your personal information, request correction of inaccurate data, or request permanent deletion of your customer records from our database. To request data erasure, simply email our Information Officer at docfloweditor@gmail.com.

Zero Tracking Guarantee: MP Market does not use invasive advertising trackers, Facebook/Meta tracking pixels, or sell customer browsing histories.
