🇿🇦 MP Market Returns & Refunds Policy

1. Compliance with the Consumer Protection Act (CPA of 2008):

Our Returns Policy operates in strict accordance with the Consumer Protection Act (CPA) and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (ECTA). Returns can be initiated within 7 to 14 days of receipt.


2. Circumstances for Returns & Exchanges:

  • Incorrectly Supplied Goods: If you receive an item substantially different from what was ordered, MP Market will collect the incorrect goods and deliver the correct item at no charge.
  • Materially Defective Goods (6-Month Statutory Warranty): If goods are found to have a manufacturing defect, they will be inspected before offering a replacement, store credit, or full refund.

3. Non-Returnable Items:

  • Goods that have been damaged due to ordinary wear and tear, willful damage, or power surges.
  • Opened beauty, skincare, personal body care items, earrings, or underwear due to public health and safety considerations.
  • Buyer's remorse (changing mind after sourcing has completed).
