Warranty & Returns

1. Consumer Protection Act (CPA) Compliance

MP Market's Returns Policy operates in accordance with the Consumer Protection Act, 2008 (CPA), and applicable South African consumer-protection legislation.

Consumers retain their statutory rights in respect of defective, unsafe or unsuitable goods.

2. Returns & Defective Goods

Incorrectly Supplied Goods:
If you receive an item that differs from what you ordered, MP Market will arrange collection and provide the correct item or an appropriate remedy at no additional delivery cost.


Defective or Faulty Goods:
Where goods are defective or fail to meet the requirements of the CPA, the applicable statutory remedies will apply. Depending on the circumstances, this may include repair, replacement or refund.

Products may be inspected to establish the nature and cause of the reported fault.


Supplier or Manufacturer Warranty:
Where a product carries an additional manufacturer or supplier warranty, the applicable warranty terms will also apply. Warranty periods may differ between products.

3. Exclusions

Returns relating to defects or damage caused by misuse, unauthorised modification or repair, negligence, accidental damage, electrical surges, or normal wear and tear may not qualify as manufacturing defects.

For hygiene and health reasons, opened cosmetics, personal-care/body-care products, earrings and underwear may be subject to applicable return restrictions, except where the product is defective or otherwise covered by a statutory consumer right.

Change-of-mind returns may be subject to the applicable provisions of the CPA and ECTA, including any applicable cooling-off or return rights.

4. Important

MP Market does not provide a separate blanket manufacturer's warranty on every product. Warranty coverage and duration may differ depending on the product and its manufacturer or supplier.

Nothing in this policy limits or excludes any consumer right that cannot lawfully be excluded under South African law.
